Academic Work
Research & Publications
Working Paper · 2025–2026
Comparative Analysis of E-VAT and Electronic Tax Systems in South Asia: Revenue Impact and Implementation Challenges with Special Reference to Bangladesh

This study examines the revenue impact and implementation challenges of E-VAT systems across six South Asian economies — Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, Nepal, Sri Lanka, and Maldives — covering FY 2019–2024. Applying TAM, Institutional Theory, and E-Governance Theory, the paper identifies why Bangladesh's VAT-to-GDP ratio of 3.6% lags regional peers despite digital platform deployment, and presents evidence-based recommendations including mandatory e-invoicing, database integration, and AI-assisted audit selection.
