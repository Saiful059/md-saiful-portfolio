Research & Publications

Applied research in tax administration and accounting compliance.

Paper 01 · E-VAT · Digital Tax Administration

E-VAT Adoption and VAT Revenue Performance in South Asia: Evidence from Bangladesh

This study examines the maturity of E-VAT systems, VAT/GST revenue performance and taxpayer behavior across selected South Asian economies, with a specific focus on Bangladesh. It proposes the Bangladesh E-VAT Adoption Framework (BEVAF), linking institutional enablers, technology platform maturity and behavioral compliance outcomes.

Keywords: E-VAT; Digital Tax Administration; VAT Compliance; Bangladesh; South Asia; TAM; E-Governance; Revenue Mobilization; SME; BEVAF
Paper 02 · Corporate Tax · IFRS Reporting

Corporate Tax Compliance Risk under IFRS-Based Financial Reporting: Multi-Case Evidence from Emerging Market Companies

This empirical accounting and taxation research explores corporate tax compliance risk under IFRS-based financial reporting in Bangladesh, combining enterprise survey evidence, listed-company annual report data and anonymized audit-work paper evidence from private companies.

Keywords: Corporate tax compliance; IFRS; effective tax rate; cash effective tax rate; book-tax differences; deferred tax; Bangladesh; emerging markets
